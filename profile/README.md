# The Korea Pulse

**https://pulse.koreasignals.com/** — English-language analysis of Korea's economy, markets and corporate disclosures.

Every article carries figures we compute ourselves from primary sources — DART corporate filings (Financial Supervisory Service) and Bank of Korea ECOS statistics — rather than restating news reports.

- Weekly DART filing digest: filing volume, type mix and filer concentration, week over week
- Macro context from ECOS series (rates, prices, housing, FX) attached where the story matches
- [Editorial standards](https://pulse.koreasignals.com/editorial-standards/)

Sister publication: [The Korea Wave](https://wave.koreasignals.com/) — the business of Korean culture.
